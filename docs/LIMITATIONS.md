# Interpretation and reproduction limits

- Cat−owl is a contrast direction, not proof that the common component represents system-prompt obedience. All teacher intervention directions were rescaled to the full teacher vector's norm.
- J and R mean the outputs of the chosen sparse nonnegative decomposition, not a complete separation of verbalizable and unverbalizable information.
- Generic readable atoms and above-random FVE occur in some student decompositions. The saved learned-shift decomposition covers original L26 seed-42 students only.
- `check_readout_only.py` historically updates the OMP residual outside its selection loop. Its teacher comparison is not matched to the J-lens solver. No corrected teacher result has been generated in this cleanup. The student readout random-baseline file is a different experiment.
- L23 fingerprint J/R contrasts decompose cat−owl; L26 contrasts decompose the full teacher direction.
- Two data/training-seed pairs do not establish an absence of transmission. The seed-43 J figure uses the latest completed rerun; an earlier result is archived, not silently pooled.
- Three neutral students give an observed range, not an uncertainty interval. Evaluation draws share 50 prompts; they are not 2,500 independent prompts.
- The released sufficiency-data filename identifies alpha 6. Our alpha-3 experiment, rule-only filter, and HF generation settings are not a direct replication of that run.
